The MIT license in [LICENSE](LICENSE) covers the **code** of this project. The files listed here are **not** covered by it: each one keeps its own license, shown below.

## Costumes (`public/assets/costumes/`)

Every costume is a fan-made 3D model downloaded from [Sketchfab](https://sketchfab.com). We converted each one to VRM and set up its rig (humanoid bone mapping and skin weights) so it follows a tracked body. Apart from that, the models are the work of the authors below.

The characters themselves are trademarks and copyrights of their respective owners. This is a non-commercial project, and it is not affiliated with or endorsed by any of them, or by the model authors. If you own one of these works and want it removed, please open an issue.

The same source, author and license are also stored inside each `.vrm` file (VRM `meta`).

The rigged Patrick Star costume is a modified version of a CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 model, so it is shared under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 as well.

## MediaPipe (`public/mediapipe/`)

The pose and gesture models (`pose_landmarker_lite.task`, `gesture_recognizer.task`) and the WebAssembly engine (`vision_wasm_*`) come from Google's [MediaPipe](https://github.com/google-ai-edge/mediapipe), licensed under the [Apache License 2.0](https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0). They are bundled unchanged so tracking works offline.
